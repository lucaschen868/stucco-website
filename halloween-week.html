Halloween Week

Oct 26–29, 2026. A week of Halloween events for the whole school.

Sign up here

Schedule

  • Homeroom Decorating Competition

    Decorate Oct 5–23 · Judging Oct 26–27

    Grades 7–10 classes turn their rooms into the most haunted room in school. Top 3 classes win prizes.

  • Spooky Ladyfingers Decoration

    Oct 26 · 12:45–13:15 · 10F Study Area

    Decorate your own ladyfingers. Sign-up required.

  • Horror Movie Showcase

    Oct 27–28 · Lunch break · 6-7 Performing Arts Classroom

    A mini movie theatre at lunch. Sign-up required.

  • Haunted Hallway

    Oct 26–29 · 6F Hallway

    Walk through our spooky hallway. Please don't touch or break the decorations.

  • Trick or Treat Stations

    Oct 26–29 · 5F–10F · Breaktime

    Hunt for little buckets of treats hidden around the school. They move each day.

  • Halloween Catwalk

    Oct 28 · 15:10–16:10 · Fu Lai Hall (2F)

    Show off your best costume on stage.

No events on Oct 25 because it's a national holiday.
